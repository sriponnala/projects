# CrewAI

This folder is about **CrewAI**, a way to run a small “crew” of AI agents. Each agent has a role (researcher, writer, coder, and so on). They follow a plan and hand work to each other.

## Projects in this folder

- **debate** — two sides argue a topic, then a decision is written up
- **coder** — agents that write and check code
- **financial_researcher** — agents that research a company or market and produce a report
- **stock_picker** — agents that look for investment ideas and explain a pick
- **engineering_team** — a lead, builders, and a tester that design and ship a small app

## Who this is for

People who want to see how a team of AIs can split a job the way a real team would: research, write, build, and review.
